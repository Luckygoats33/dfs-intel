## Per-fight summary
All facts are from the UFC.com preview unless noted. I = inference.
1. **Moicano vs Nolan (LW, 5 rds).** Facts: Moicano submitted Chris Duncan in R2 in April 2026. His recent wins are over Duncan, Saint Denis and Turner; losses to Makhachev and Dariush. Nolan is 26, has won 5 straight with his only UFC loss in his debut, and his last win was a UD over Ziam. He has R1 finishes of Charlie Campbell and Victor Martinez. Market: 53/47. I: close to a coin flip. Nolan's 5-round cardio is untested. DFS: 5 rounds raise the ceiling for both, and Moicano's sub/grappling path gives him a higher floor (I).
2. **Brown vs Smith (WW).** Facts: Smith is unbeaten on the DWCS/UFC run with 4 finishes in 4 UFC-era fights (3 KOs). He's a former Oklahoma State wrestler on short notice. Brown went 8-2 from 2021–25 with KOs of Dalby and Salikhov, then lost a decision to Holland in 2026. Market: Smith 70%. I: Smith has finish upside. Short notice plus a wrestle-heavy style suggests a strong DFS ceiling for him.
3. **Osbourne vs Durden (FLW).** Facts: Osbourne is 1-2 in his last 3. Durden is 1-5 in his last 6. They have 22 combined finishes. Market: 49/51. I: high volatility. Weak spot for parlays; contrarian DFS spot.
4. **Yana vs Luana Santos (W-BW).** Facts: Yana is 36 on a 3-fight win streak that includes Tate. Luana is on a 3-fight win streak over Rosa, Croden and Lisboa. Market: invalid data. I: no lean without valid odds.
5. **Lebosnoyani vs Hasanov (WW).** Facts: Lebosnoyani is 2-0 in the UFC, both decisions. Hasanov (ATT) is 1-0 in the UFC with a win over Eric Nolan. Market: Lebosnoyani 71%. I: decision lean, which caps his DFS ceiling.
6. **Marshall vs Hope (FW).** Facts: Marshall submitted Silva inside R1 and beat Brennan. Hope lost a UD to Kamaka III in his debut. Hope is a replacement. Market: Marshall 66%.
7. **Sezinando vs Berggren (WW).** Facts: this is Sezinando's UFC debut after the TUF 33 final loss to Donchenko, about 1 year off. Berggren has a 100% finish rate and his only UFC loss is to Donchenko. Market: Sezinando 62%. I: Berggren's finishing profile makes him a DFS dog to consider.
8. **Erosa vs Lee (FW).** Facts: Erosa has gone the distance 1 time in his last 7 and has 3 finishes in his last 5. Lee won Road to UFC S1 and has 2 losses and 3 cancellations since. Market: Lee 59%. I: likely action/finish fight.
9. **Reese vs Gall (MW).** Facts: Gall has lost 4 straight and has been out since Nov 2024 (about 2 years). Reese has gone the distance 3 times in 9 UFC fights and lost his last 2. Market: Reese 75%. I: Reese finish upside.
10. **Klein vs Kropschot (MW).** Market: Kropschot 73%. Facts: not covered in the UFC preview. Stats missing.
11. **Alencar vs Rodriguez (W-SW).** Facts: Rodriguez has 3 straight decisions (Hughes, Souza, Knutsson). Alencar has a submission of Carnelossi (preview video title). Market: Rodriguez 70%. I: decision lean, so a lower DFS ceiling.
12. **Szabova vs Lisboa (W-FLW).** Facts: Szabova is 10-0 with 7 finishes, a former Oktagon 2-division champion, and this is her UFC debut. Lisboa has 3 KOs, 3 subs, 3 R1 finishes, and is on 2 straight losses. Market: Szabova 85%. I: the chalk; finish plausible.
